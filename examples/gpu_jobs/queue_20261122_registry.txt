# The registry check (PI 2026-11-22: "MI100 isn't online go on runpod"; docs/MLIP_SECOND_OPINION.md 3.24).
#
# 3.23 found by accident that the film composition's all-trans cell with its neighbours a monomer along the
# chains is 0.84 kcal/mol per chain below the upright cell in DFT, the cell every margin is measured from: its
# margin is +0.063 from the one and +0.123 from the other.  Every other cell has been seen in one registry.
# This pod makes the other registries on purpose (examples/registry_starts.sh: 34 starts from the four
# cells' labelled all-trans and helix cells), relaxes them in the potential on the card, four side by side,
# to 0.004 eV/A (600 steps a cell at most, 45 minutes in all: the starts are in the order they matter, and what
# is not at rest by then is left out), and puts the lowest of each composition and
# conformation to DFT on two lines: the card's (46 minutes a cell) and the processors' (66).  While the card
# relaxes, the processors build the DFT program for the card and then label the image after the leader's
# second top, which the last pod was deleted 45 minutes into.
#
# One job a line; the pod counts lines, so the file is only added to.
registry_card.sh docs/predictions/mlip/registry_starts.extxyz 4 600 2700 docs/predictions/mlip/paths/leader_b2.extxyz leader_b2_i08
#
# 07:08 UTC.  The first cells of the job above: nine minutes a cell at 600 steps, and three of the first
# four are not at rest (the matrix's all-trans cell in the film's registry +2.06 kcal/mol per chain above
# its upright cell, the nitrile cell's +0.54, the leader's +5.38; the film composition's helix with its
# neighbours two monomers along at rest, +3.15).  A cell that is not at rest says little: the film
# composition's own low registry, built the same way and relaxed here on four processors, is still 2.1
# above its upright cell at 250 steps on its way to 1.27 below.  So when the job above ends, the cells
# that are not at rest and are less than 4 above their labelled cells are relaxed on from where they
# stand, the lowest first, 900 more steps each and 40 minutes in all; then the table and the choice
# again (a cell not at rest is put to DFT only if it is already 0.3 below its labelled cell).
registry_more.sh docs/predictions/mlip/registry_starts.extxyz 4 900 2400 auto:4.0
#
# 07:13 UTC.  Why the cells above are slow: the lattice.  The film composition's low cell is 5% narrower along
# one cell edge and 5% wider along the other than its upright cell, and a start made in the upright lattice
# takes hundreds of steps to get there.  So the film composition's low cell is given to the other three
# compositions whole (examples/registry_starts_like.sh: the same turn of the chains, the same strain of the
# lattice across them, the same monomers along), and to the film's composition itself as the control: its
# cell must come back to the one 3.23 labelled, 1.27 below upright.  Four cells, one a process, ten minutes.
registry_card.sh docs/predictions/mlip/registry_starts_like.extxyz 4 600 900
#
# 07:34 UTC.  The control, run here on four processors while the card works: the film composition's cell made
# like its own low cell starts 13 above its upright cell (rigid upright chains in the low cell's lattice) and
# is 0.7 above at 325 steps, still falling 0.4 a hundred steps toward the 1.27 below: these cells need about a
# thousand steps whatever the start.  Of the first job's cells at rest so far, every helix cell with its
# neighbours two monomers along is above its upright helix cell (1.7 to 5.8).  So the card's time goes to
# the cells made like the film composition's low cell: the second job above is cut to ten minutes
# (examples/gpu_jobs/registry_more.cap: a queue line cannot be changed), and after the third the like cells
# are relaxed on, 900 more steps each, 20 minutes.
registry_more.sh docs/predictions/mlip/registry_starts_like.extxyz 4 900 1200 auto:6.0
#
# 07:34 UTC.  So that the processors' line does not stand idle when the image after the leader's second top
# is done: two cells of 3.23 that are relaxed and have no label.  The leader's all-trans cell in its
# sheet's registry (1.50 above its upright cell in the potential; DFT has it only as the scan left it), and
# the film composition's upright cell with its chains turned and nothing else (level with upright in the
# potential, which is the whole of the claim that the 0.84 is the registry and not the turn).
dft_cells.sh start 8 2 docs/predictions/mlip/paths/all_trans_tight.extxyz leader_r_released_tight film_all_trans_turned_117_tight
#
# 07:44 UTC.  What the control showed, and what follows from it.  The film composition's cell made like its
# own low cell (the same turn, lattice and registry, from upright chains) stopped falling 0.6 above its
# upright cell at 450 steps, 1.9 above the low cell it was made like.  The two differ in the chains
# themselves: an all-trans chain's bonds are each twisted 5 to 20 degrees off trans, and the pattern of those
# twists along the chain is another one in the low cell (which got it on the way back from a kink) than in
# the upright cell.  So a registry is not found by moving upright chains, and the cells of the third job
# above are of little use (the fourth is cut to a minute).  The low cell itself is the start now
# (examples/registry_starts_as_film.sh): with its chlorine made fluorine it is the matrix, sequence and all,
# in the film composition's low cell (1.6 above the matrix's upright cell before any relaxing); with its
# neighbours moved back a monomer, and with the upright cell's whole lattice given back, it says whether the
# 0.84 is the registry or the chains (7.7 and 8.6 above upright before relaxing: the twists fit the registry
# they are in).  Four cells, one a process, 1000 steps at most, 25 minutes.
registry_card.sh docs/predictions/mlip/registry_starts_as_film.extxyz 4 1000 1500
#
# 07:51 UTC.  Of the first 34 starts, 19 are at rest now, every one above its upright cell: the all-trans
# cells with their neighbours a monomer along 0.45 to 2.3 above (the nitrile cell's nearest), the helix cells
# with theirs two monomers along 1.4 to 5.8 above.  Chains with the upright cell's twists do not like another
# registry.  One more cell made of the film composition's low cell: its CFE unit made an acrylonitrile unit
# (the nitrile where the chlorine was: the hand the labelled nitrile cell has), which is the nitrile cell,
# sequence and all, in the film composition's low cell; and the same moved back to the upright registry.
registry_card.sh docs/predictions/mlip/registry_starts_as_film_nitrile.extxyz 2 1000 1200
#
# 08:09 UTC.  The four cells made of the film composition's low cell are at rest (the count three comments up
# should read 18 at rest, not 19).  In the potential, kcal/mol per chain against each composition's own
# upright cell: THE MATRIX IN THE FILM COMPOSITION'S LOW CELL IS 1.50 BELOW ITS UPRIGHT CELL (the film
# composition's own low cell: 1.27 below).  The low cell's chains with their neighbours moved back to the
# upright registry rest 0.18 above upright, and in the upright lattice and turn 0.54 above; upright chains in
# the low cell's lattice and registry rested 0.57 above.  So the low cell is its chains' twists AND its
# registry together, neither alone, and it is not the chlorine's: the matrix has it too.  The job put the
# matrix's cell on the card's DFT line itself.  What is left open is the other half of every margin: a helix
# cell may have a lower state of the same hidden kind, and nothing so far could find one.  So the four
# labelled helix cells are shaken (1.5 ps at 350 K in the potential, each in its own cell) and relaxed again;
# one that comes to rest more than 0.3 below where it started goes to DFT.
