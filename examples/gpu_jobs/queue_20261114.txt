# The single-chain step of the terpolymer and of its matrix in four and in sixteen chains
# (PI, 2026-11-14: "Go").  A queue for examples/runpod_jobs_pod.sh: one job per line, run in
# order, each at the branch's commit of the moment; lines are only ever added while a pod
# follows it.  (A first pod ran job 1 and was deleted 98 s in: the second line was then the
# four-chain PVDF row, 240 charged sites, which the code keeps on the host; 25 minutes of a
# rented card idle.  That control had already been run on a host before the pod, and is not
# queued any more; the sixteen-chain control takes its bands' start from the stored row.)
#
# 1. the evaluation: the batched form on the card against the host's loops, on the three rows'
#    own cells of four and sixteen chains (PVDF is the control: its tests are on PVDF).
forms_check.sh vdf9-trfe4-cfe1-14 vdf10-trfe4-14 vdf10
# 2. the terpolymer, 64/29/7: four chains (with the check of the sheet), then sixteen with each
#    band started from the four-chain one.
band_row.sh vdf9-trfe4-cfe1-14 2 1
band_row.sh vdf9-trfe4-cfe1-14 4 2 from 2x1 tiled
# 3. its matrix, 71/29.
band_row.sh vdf10-trfe4-14 2 1
band_row.sh vdf10-trfe4-14 4 2 from 2x1 tiled
# 4. the protocol at sixteen chains: PVDF from nothing, against the stored host row (I +9.963,
#    B +5.911, saddles +10.660 and +11.637; its bands took 2.7 hours on the host).
band_row.sh vdf10 4 2 from 2x1 stored tiled
# 5. the terpolymer's second band had not converged in its 300 iterations (four chains: top
#    +14.33 at a force of 0.31 and falling, and the saddle search from it slid off the path).
#    The same rows again: a stored band that has not converged is continued, a converged one kept.
band_row.sh vdf9-trfe4-cfe1-14 2 1 tiled
band_row.sh vdf9-trfe4-cfe1-14 4 2 from 2x1 tiled
# 6. the saddle search from the four-chain I-B band's top did not slide at random: it fell into a
#    third state of the moving chain, M (one sharp gauche bond, its partner spread over two bonds;
#    +11.20 between I +9.04 and B +13.00; docs/predictions/kink_states_belt4.json), which the band
#    from I to B jumps over.  Bands through it, in four chains and (M looked for with the moving
#    chain's coordinates from the four-chain M) in sixteen; and where the sixteen-chain search
#    ended (N: gauche bonds at 4 and 6, at or under +8.9 before relaxing).
between.sh vdf9-trfe4-cfe1-14 2 1 I.state settled:M I-M
between.sh vdf9-trfe4-cfe1-14 2 1 settled:M B.state M-B
settle.sh vdf9-trfe4-cfe1-14 4 2 I.state M mover 2x1 M
between.sh vdf9-trfe4-cfe1-14 4 2 I.state settled:M I-M
between.sh vdf9-trfe4-cfe1-14 4 2 settled:M B.state M-B
settle.sh vdf9-trfe4-cfe1-14 4 2 I-B.polished.state N
# 7. the matrix the same way.  Its four-chain second band, given 900 iterations, found a dip by
#    itself (image 6, +11.75) and its saddle settled at +13.68; its sixteen-chain band converged
#    without one, on a saddle at +15.00.  The dip relaxed and named (M), looked for in sixteen
#    chains, and the bands through it there.
settle.sh vdf10-trfe4-14 2 1 I-B.images.6 M
settle.sh vdf10-trfe4-14 4 2 I.state M mover 2x1 M
between.sh vdf10-trfe4-14 4 2 I.state settled:M I-M
between.sh vdf10-trfe4-14 4 2 settled:M B.state M-B
# 8. where the terpolymer's sixteen-chain search had ended is a minimum too: N, a kink pair of the
#    other hand two monomers on (gauche bonds 4 and 6, G- T G+), +8.82, under I (+9.05) and B
#    (+11.79).  The way to it from I; and whether the matrix and PVDF have one (looked for from I
#    with the trailing gauche bond set trans and bond 6 set gauche), and their way to it.
between.sh vdf9-trfe4-cfe1-14 4 2 I.state settled:N I-N
settle.sh vdf10-trfe4-14 4 2 I.state N torsions 0:180,6:100
settle.sh vdf10 4 2 I.state N torsions 0:180,6:100
between.sh vdf10-trfe4-14 4 2 I.state settled:N I-N
between.sh vdf10 4 2 I.state settled:N I-N
# 9. the terpolymer's band from I to N had not converged in 300 iterations (top +13.50 at a force
#    of 0.41, a dip beside it): once more, then the pod removes itself.
between.sh vdf9-trfe4-cfe1-14 4 2 I.state settled:N I-N
stop
