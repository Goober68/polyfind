# The queue of the PI's MI100 machine (examples/mi100_runner.sh): one job a line, run in order,
# append-only (the runner counts lines).  A line is a script of this folder and its arguments.
#
# 1. What the machine is, and whether the card, the potential and DFT give the stored numbers on it.
mi100_check.sh
# 2. The urea row's wall stepping on by a travelling twist, in the potential on the card, in cells
#    six and eight repeats long (216 and 288 atoms): how the pair of twists' cost depends on how
#    far apart the two are.  The four-repeat cell was done on the other machine's processors.
triage.sh kink --names urea1_tt_x1 --device cuda --long 6 --turned 2,3,4
triage.sh kink --names urea1_tt_x1 --device cuda --long 8 --turned 2,3,4,5,6
# 3. The four-repeat states relaxed in DFT on the processors, the cell held (144 atoms a state):
#    the pair of twists by DFT's own structures and not the potential's.
triage.sh points --names urea1_tt_x1_kink4 --relax
# 4. (added 2026-11-25, after the four-repeat states were read in DFT on the other machine: the
#    part-turned state is no hump, the unit that changes sides is one repeat, and what the two
#    whole states differ by is the row's width.)  Wider rows, so that a wall steps with no other
#    wall beside it: six and eight chains, four repeats long (216 and 288 atoms), every count of
#    repeats turned, in the potential on the card.
triage.sh kink --names urea1_tt_x1 --device cuda --rows 6 --long 4 --turned 4,3,2,1,0
triage.sh kink --names urea1_tt_x1 --device cuda --rows 8 --long 4 --turned 4,3,2,1,0
# 5. (added 2026-11-25, after the hump for the first urea to change sides was read in DFT both
#    ways round in the cell one sheet thick: 23.6 and 24.7 kcal/mol, at a contact between the
#    turning urea and its own copy in the next sheet.)  The same step in a row two sheets thick
#    (288 atoms), where the urea above stays as it is: the row at rest, then one carbonyl taken
#    round both ways, in the potential on the card.  (Job 3's states with 3, 2 and 1 turned were
#    made by twisting and are upper marks: the state with one urea turned where it stands is 11
#    lower in DFT.  Its relaxations are still worth having for the two whole states.)
triage.sh kink --names urea1_tt_x1 --device cuda --sheets 2 --long 4 --turned 4 --only
triage.sh swing --names urea1_tt_x1 --device cuda --sheets 2 --long 4 --start turned_4 --carbonyl 0 --by 171 --way p,m --images 9
